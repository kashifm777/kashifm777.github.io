About

I'm an ML engineer working on large language models, generative AI and the systems that carry them into production. I architect and deploy scalable ML services in TensorFlow and PyTorch, and I work on model compression and reinforcement learning for problems where resources are tight.

I've built autonomous driving modules, computer vision running on headless edge devices, and retrieval pipelines over messy enterprise data. My MS thesis at NED covered knowledge distillation for compressing LLMs small enough to run at the edge. I write about what I learn, mostly retrieval and fine-tuning.
